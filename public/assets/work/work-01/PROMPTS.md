### film/cyber-walk-tvc-15sec.mpg
A real 15-second TVC (raw video, silent aside from its own audio) for a Gurgaon office-park
brand called Cyberwalk — aerial/CG establishing shots of the development.
**Prompt:** "Take a representative frame from this TVC and composite it as the screen content
of a slim modern flatscreen TV mounted in a dim, minimal navy-toned studio (#262970 walls),
ambient gold (#c79a5c) accent lighting either side of the screen, shallow reflection on a dark
floor. Alternatively, re-grade the clip itself with a cooler navy shadow tone and crop to a
21:9 cinematic letterboxed frame with subtle film grain."

### ooh/gantry-suraj-kund-road.jpg
Real street photo of a road-gantry hoarding ("Silk — cuisine from the great orient") over a

**Prompt:** "Isolate the gantry/billboard structure from this photo and re-render it holding a

a gold (#c79a5c) underline rule. Keep the real street environment (road, motorbike, trees, dusk
sky) around it for scale and authenticity, shot from below looking up, warm dusk light, light
traffic motion-blur in the foreground."
